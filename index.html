Pairing and button controls
  1. Pair on the surfaceHold both buttons for 3 seconds. Synchronized LEDs are the proposed pairing indication; pair-code handling remains open.
  2. Call the buddyA short press on A requests three strong buzzes on B. A packet reply would not prove the person noticed.
  3. Request distance feedbackA 1-second hold requests ranging. Both bands exchange messages; A receives the distance cadence. B’s ongoing indication is undecided.
  4. Stop the sessionOne press on either band requests Stop. The proposed interface includes a 5-minute timeout and a 1-second burst then stop below 2 m; proximity does not confirm reunion.

Sealed charging

A receiver coil transfers power through the sealed cap, avoiding an exposed charging connector on the band. The charging interface remains enclosed during normal use.

The trade-off is a dedicated dock, alignment and thermal design, plus an approximately two-hour charge target. Inductive charging alone does not establish waterproofing or eliminate all sealing risks.

A module that stays with you

The module is intended to release from the silicone cradle so the band can be replaced. The cap remains sealed. Retention, service method and battery replacement policy still need definition.

Small by design.

The nominal housing is 38 × 28 × 11.5 mm, excluding the button protrusion. Fit, clearances and manufacturing tolerances remain under engineering review.

These Rev B concept views use the existing source geometry. The original DBA-001 sheet shows 88.5 mm overall height; the source gives 84.7 mm including the button. The nominal 11.5 mm housing excludes the 2.2 mm modeled button protrusion. No tolerance or pressure rating is released.

Engineering review notes
  • Internal clearance: the coil extends beyond the cap envelope in the supplied source layout; the battery and button/switch arrangement also need reconciliation.
  • Energy arithmetic: the stated state currents yield about 5.5 mAh over the stated listening/ranging durations before omitted loads, not the PDF’s roughly 100 mAh. Neither number establishes the runtime.
  • Drawing authority: the original sheet’s overall height, blanket tolerance and scale must not be treated as released manufacturing requirements. No manufacturing tolerances have been released.

Designed as a pair.

Two bands. One shared dock.

The proposed kit serves recreational buddy pairs, with instructors and dive shops as potential sales channels. The retail target is $179–199 per pair.

The desk estimate is about $86 per pair at 5,000 pairs, ±30%, including the dock allocation. Tooling, engineering, freight and duty are excluded. Supplier quotes and channel margins remain open.
